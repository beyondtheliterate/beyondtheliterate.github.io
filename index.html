Beyond the Literate

Where stories inspire, creativity flourishes, and communities grow.

Welcome to Beyond the Literate, a youth-led community that brings together students who share a passion for literature, creativity, and meaningful connections. Through books, collaborative projects, and engaging events, we aim to create a space where every member can learn, contribute, and grow.

What we do

More Than a Book Club

At Beyond the Literate, we believe that reading is only the beginning. Every book opens new ideas, every event creates new memories, and every design tells a story. Our committees work together to create a welcoming environment where young people can explore their interests, develop new skills, and connect with others who share their passions.

About us

The story behind the pages

Our Story
Our Story

How it began

Beyond the Literate began with a simple idea: to create a space where young people could connect through shared interests and build a supportive community together.

What started as a small initiative quickly grew into a place where members can exchange ideas, collaborate on creative projects, and inspire one another through literature, design, and community events.

Our Mission
Our Mission

What drives us

Our mission is to empower young people by providing opportunities to learn, create, and connect through meaningful experiences. We strive to build an inclusive community where curiosity is encouraged, creativity is celebrated, and every member feels welcome.

Our Goal
Our Goal

Where we're headed

At Beyond the Literate, our goal is to build a welcoming youth community where students can learn, create, and grow together. We believe that books have the power to spark ideas, but true growth comes from sharing those ideas, collaborating with others, and turning inspiration into action.

Through literature, creative design, and meaningful events, we hope to encourage curiosity, build confidence, and create lasting friendships that extend beyond the pages of a book.

Committees

Three committees, one community

Committee 01

Books Committee

The Books Committee is at the heart of Beyond the Literate. Each week, our team selects a book to recommend to the community. Alongside every recommendation, we share a summary, genre, themes, reading benefits, and the reasons why we believe it's worth exploring.

Whether you're an experienced reader or just beginning your reading journey, our goal is to help you discover stories that inspire, educate, and encourage meaningful conversations.

Committee 02

Design Committee

The Design Committee is responsible for bringing our community's ideas to life visually. From event posters and social media graphics to branding and creative projects, this committee helps shape the identity of Beyond the Literate.

Members have the opportunity to collaborate on designs, develop new creative skills, and contribute to every event we organize.

Committee 03

Event Planning Committee

The Event Planning Committee organizes the experiences that bring our community together. From discussions and workshops to social gatherings and community activities, this committee works to create engaging events where members can learn, collaborate, and have fun.

Every event is planned with the goal of building friendships, encouraging participation, and creating memorable experiences.
